# Profile illustration

Built-in image generation tool.

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: compact panoramic pixel illustration beneath a personal GitHub profile.
Primary request: original, elegant Greek architectural pixel art for Will Sims. A quiet fragment of an ancient Ionic stoa seen in side elevation, weathered pale marble, a low stone terrace, and a little olive foliage. Distinct architectural silhouettes, restrained detail, and generous negative space.
Composition: extremely wide panoramic 3:1 composition. Architecture occupies the right two thirds, while low steps and a quiet sky extend to the left. Keep the entire structure visible with breathing room. Three columns with convincing Ionic capitals, one partly broken. Low horizon. An intimate architectural vignette rather than a monumental landscape or website hero.
Style: carefully hand-placed 2D pixel art, a limited warm monochrome palette, crisp square pixels, selective dithering, elegant editorial composition. Pale stone against a deep charcoal night sky with faint cloud bands and a few small stars. Restrained sage foliage. Quiet moonlight, without showing a giant moon.
Constraints: no text, names, slogans, interface, data, calendars, grids, repeated miniature columns, snakes, light trails, people, logos, badges, borders, or frames. No mountain valley flanked by two giant foreground columns. No photorealism, smooth 3D, anime, neon, or game HUD.

Preserve the exact panoramic 3:1 composition, architecture, three Ionic columns, stone terrace, tree and foliage positions, shoreline, camera, and pixel-art texture.
